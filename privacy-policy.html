Sayday – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains what information the Sayday Android app (“the App”, Turkish name “Sayday”) collects, how it is used and what your rights are. The App is developed by Ersen Öztürk (“we”).

1. In short

  • Your notes, plans, reminders, income/expense entries, offers and sections are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to our servers.
  • For your account we use only your e-mail address and password, held by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Voice recordings are not stored. Only the sentence transcribed from your voice is saved on your phone.
  • The free version shows Google AdMob ads (see 4a). Sayday Premium subscribers see no ads.
  • Your notes, plans and expenses are never used for advertising, and your data is not sold.

3. Microphone and voice

  • Background listening (optional, turned on in Settings): audio is transcribed entirely on the device with a Vosk speech model downloaded to your phone. Audio is never sent anywhere. While it is on, Android shows a persistent notification and a microphone indicator; you can turn it off at any time.
  • Microphone button / home-screen widget: your phone’s own speech-recognition service is used (Google on most devices), under that service’s privacy terms. The App never stores the audio; it only receives the transcribed text.

The speech model is downloaded once from alphacephei.com; during that download only standard connection data (e.g. IP address) reaches that server.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase Authentication – e-mail sign-in and verification. Firebase privacy information
  • Google Play services – required by Firebase.
  • Google AdMob and Google Play Billing – ads and subscription (see 4a). Google Privacy Policy
  • Google ML Kit (Entity Extraction) – for languages other than Turkish, finds dates, times and amounts in your sentence on the device. A small model for the chosen language is downloaded once from Google; your text is not sent.
  • Your device’s speech-recognition and text-to-speech services – only for the microphone button and spoken confirmations.

4a. Ads and subscription

  • Ads (free version): Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), and device and app information. Your notes, voice and entries are not sent to AdMob. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form appears before ads; you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s Settings → Privacy → Ads. How Google uses data from partner apps
  • Sayday Premium: a monthly or yearly subscription for ad-free use. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card details. The subscription renews automatically and can be cancelled anytime in Play Store → Subscriptions.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone: to take voice notes.
  • Notifications: for reminders and alarms.
  • Alarms & reminders / full-screen notifications: so reminders ring on time and on the lock screen.
  • Internet: for sign-in/verification and the one-time download of speech and language models.
  • Run at startup: to re-schedule your alarms after the phone restarts.

6. Retention and deletion

  • On-device entries stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the App.
  • You can delete your account in the App: Settings (⚙) → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your Firebase account and all entries on the phone.
  • If you cannot access the App, e-mail ersenoztrk@gmail.com from your account’s address with the subject “Account deletion”. Requests are completed within 30 days.

6a. Backups

  • On-device automatic backup: Every day the App keeps a copy of your entries on your phone, in the App’s private storage (last 7 days).
  • Android backup: If Google backup is turned on on your phone, Android backs up your entries and settings to your own Google account and restores them when you set up a new or reset phone. This backup is managed by Google; we cannot see it.
  • Backup to a file: In Settings → Backup you can save your entries as a file wherever you choose (e.g. Google Drive, WhatsApp) and restore it later.
  • Google Drive backup (optional): If you turn it on, your entries are saved daily to a “Sayday Yedekleri” (Sayday Backups) folder in your own Google Drive (last 30 backups kept). The App uses only the drive.file permission for this: it can see only the folder and files it created itself and cannot access any other file in your Drive. Backups go directly from your phone to your Google Drive; no data is sent to the developer. You can turn this off at any time and remove the permission in your Google Account under “Third-party connections”.
  • Sayday’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Security

Sign-in data is transmitted to Google Firebase over encrypted connections (HTTPS). On-device entries are kept in Android’s app-private storage and cannot be read by other apps.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, Turkey’s KVKK or California’s CCPA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data and to object to its processing. To exercise these rights, write to ersenoztrk@gmail.com. The legal basis for processing your e-mail address is the performance of the service you requested (your account).

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and the “Last updated” date will change.
